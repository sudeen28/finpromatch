What a Nigerian tax investigation involves

A FIRS tax investigation typically starts with a notice requesting records, followed by a review of filing history, bank statements, and supporting documentation across one or more tax years. Depending on what the investigation uncovers, it can escalate from a routine desk audit into a full field audit, and in serious cases, into assessment disputes that require formal representation. The process is document-heavy, time-sensitive, and highly procedural, which is exactly where the wrong choice of accountant costs businesses the most.

Why a general accountant often isn't enough

Many businesses in Nigeria default to whichever accountant already handles their bookkeeping when a FIRS notice arrives. That's frequently a mistake. A tax investigation calls for someone who understands FIRS procedure specifically: how assessments are computed, what documentation actually satisfies an auditor, and when it's appropriate to formally object to a demand notice. A tax consultant in Lagos or elsewhere who does this work regularly will typically move faster and produce a cleaner outcome than a firm handling its first investigation.
