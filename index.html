Location matters, sometimes

Local when it matters. Remote when it doesn't.

For engagements that need someone on the ground, we prioritize firms based in or near your location. For work that can be handled remotely, geography matters a lot less, and that's factored into which firms we suggest.

On-site workInvestigations, audits with physical records, and matters requiring an in-person presence get matched to firms who can actually show up, starting with firms in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

Frequently asked questions

A few questions you might have.

01 How does FinProMatch work? +
Tell us what you're dealing with: the type of work, your industry, location, and how sensitive it is. Our team reviews the request personally and identifies which specialist firm is the right fit, then makes the introduction. There's also a separate, lighter-weight option to work with an individual professional directly for smaller, simpler work.
02 What's the difference between getting matched with a firm and finding an individual professional? +
Firm matching is for engagements where specialisation, location, and capacity genuinely matter: tax investigations, forensic accounting, audits, asset tracing, restructuring and similar work. The individual professional track is a lighter option for smaller, routine work (like a single filing), and includes an optional contract-and-payment process through FinProMatch. Both are manually reviewed by our team; use whichever fits what you need.
03 How are professionals and firms verified? +
We check identity, professional body registration (ICAN, ANAN, CITN, or equivalent), and relevant experience, for individual professionals and firms alike. It's a manual review by our team, and for firms we track it at a granular level (identity, credentials, and even specific specialisations) rather than one blanket approval.
04 How do I choose an individual professional? +
After you submit a request on the individual-professional track, we send you two to three matched professionals with their experience and specialisations. You pick the one that fits and we set up the contract. Firm matching works differently: FinProMatch's team makes the introduction rather than presenting you with a list to choose from.
05 How does payment protection work? +
This applies to the individual-professional track: you pay FinProMatch, not the professional directly, and that payment is held until the work is marked delivered, then released minus our commission. It's not a regulated escrow service. It's a payment process designed to reduce the risk of paying upfront with no recourse. Firm engagements are currently introduction-only; FinProMatch doesn't yet process payment between you and the firm.
06 What happens if I'm unhappy with the work? +
Tell us and we'll re-match you at no extra cost, whether that was a firm or an individual professional. We're also building a more structured dispute process as the platform grows.
07 What services can I request? +
Everything from routine bookkeeping, tax filing (PAYE, VAT, CIT), and payroll setup, through to tax investigations and disputes, forensic accounting, fraud investigation, asset tracing, litigation support, audits, and due diligence. If your need doesn't fit neatly into one of those, tell us anyway and we'll find the right fit.
08 Can businesses use FinProMatch, not just individuals? +
Yes, most requests come from small and growing businesses that need ongoing tax and accounting support, not a one-off filing.
09 How do professionals or firms join? +
Individual professionals apply through the professional portal with their registration number, expertise, and experience. Accounting and advisory firms register through 'List My Firm' instead, with details for the whole practice. Both are manually reviewed before you start receiving matched requests.
10 When do I pay? +
Only on the individual-professional track, and only after you've chosen a matched professional and signed the one-page contract. Nothing is charged before that. Firm engagements don't currently have a payment step through FinProMatch.
11 How does FinProMatch make money? +
We take a commission on individual-professional engagements, and on renewals or repeat work for a period after the introduction.
