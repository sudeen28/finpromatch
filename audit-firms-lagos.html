Why capacity matters as much as credentials

Two audit firms can both be properly ICAN-registered and still be completely mismatched to a given engagement. A firm sized for small-business statutory audits may not have the bandwidth for a multi-entity corporate audit with a tight regulatory deadline, and a larger firm built for enterprise clients may be needlessly expensive for a straightforward annual audit. FinProMatch factors your business size and the nature of the audit into the match, not just whether a firm is generally "qualified."

Types of audit work covered

Firms on FinProMatch are matched against statutory annual audits, corporate audits for regulatory or investor purposes, internal control reviews, and audits tied to specific compliance requirements. If your need doesn't map neatly onto one of those categories, our team still reviews the request and finds an appropriate fit.

What to prepare before an audit engagement

Most audits move faster when financial statements, bank reconciliations, and supporting documentation for the period under review are organized in advance. An audit firm matched to your business will typically confirm the exact document list at the start of the engagement, but having records in reasonable order beforehand shortens the process and often reduces cost, since less of the engagement is spent chasing down paperwork.

Lagos-based, with reach beyond Lagos

Many audit engagements benefit from an in-person presence, particularly where physical records or site visits are involved, which is why FinProMatch prioritizes matching you with an audit firm already based in Lagos, or with a genuine presence in Lagos, before looking further afield. Remote-friendly engagements aren't restricted the same way.
